## Original effects

`SFX/KunaiThrow.wav`, `FireballCast.wav`, `WaveStart.wav`, `Victory.wav`, and
`Defeat.wav` are original synthesized effects created for this project, without
third-party samples. They are dedicated under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (see the same

longer low whoosh/crackle. Round cues use short original ascending/descending notes.
